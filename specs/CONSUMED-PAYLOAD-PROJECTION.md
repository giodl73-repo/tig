Scope: optional pure recipe policy plus explicit native claim replay, synthetic
and Git/archive fixtures. No cache initialization, media IO, daemon, consumer
switch, new dependency, source/job adoption or eviction. L0: structure/fmt/lint/
standalone; L1: exact subset/role/order/policy/budget/privacy and legacy regression;
L2: context/claim replay with retained controls and detached Git. Producer review
is not independent acceptance. Full readiness and hosted qualification stay open.

The graph/context captures everything required to interpret the source. A producer
may consume only part of those captured payloads. Add an opt-in recipe entry point
with an independently supplied policy for EVERY admitted definition: selected
document yes/no, and exact stream slots. No default inheritance or implicit empty
policy. Validate definition pins, unique policies, slot bounds and known selector
membership before extra source reads where possible. For each exact bound record,
verify requested slots actually exist and satisfy the graph's captured selector
(named slots or role/group predicates). Same-byte aliases do not grant access to
another uncaptured slot/role. A requested selected object requires captured
selected-object admission. Missing/unknown/partial/unsupported policies refuse.

Keep full graph checkpoint/binding/admission, production requirements, original
field/default rules and all Production edges unchanged. Only recipe payload
consumption narrows. Hash explicit projected selector semantics and selected/
ordered slot-role-group-object bindings under a distinct experimental projection
identity; consumption-policy identity records the entire admitted policy. Preserve
legacy entry point/wire identities. Projected and legacy plans are intentionally
different identities even if they happen to consume the same payloads.

A container control can remain required/retained while its raw bytes are excluded
from consumed identity, provided the admitted producer supplies all actual consumed
semantic meaning through declared fields/operation parameters. This API cannot
prove that extraction or producer honesty. It never omits graph vertices/edges,
interprets arbitrary JSON payloads, changes eligibility/approval, or makes a native
authoring fingerprint a complete recipe. Native projection and parameter/runtime
contracts require separate qualification. Outputs still cannot enter Production.

Native produced-claim replay gets a separate explicit projected-policy function.
Recompute the original context/plan under external admission and check claim
consumption identity; never infer a projection from the claim or silently downgrade
to legacy replay. Context/job retention continues using the full captured graph.

Pre-code producer lenses: identity requires structural slot membership, not merely
payload-set equality; privacy keeps errors free of IDs/private values; Git avoids
a second authored inventory; recovery requires exact external original policy;
placement/custody forbid treating an ignored recipe control as evictable. All
policies/records remain bounded and unsafe/conflicting sizes still refuse.
