## Why a separate protocol

The existing bounded bulk controller preserves selected bindings of held objects.
If more than MAX_ROOTS historical roots share bytes, every bounded batch sees the
other roots as protection. Repeating batches cannot settle that whole selection.
Keep those existing semantics. The larger protocol independently authorizes
retirement of exact selected historical claims and final removal of an immutable
eligible copy set. Current/new/recent/foreign/unselected obligations survive.

```text
exact whole selection + coherent complete source basis
  -> immutable asset-side selection/protection manifest
  -> independently admitted claim-settlement and final-copy decision
  -> durably acknowledged intent and bounded page/recovery admissions
  -> exact historical-claim pages with proved survivor lineage
  -> original eligible copy actions, only after their selected aliases settle
  -> live clearance/provider/authority/window fences and honest terminal effects
```

Selecting historical claims and deleting bytes have different effects. Claim
settlement may retire selected history while another admitted obligation keeps
the bytes. It does not release provider grants. The separately approved copy set
excludes originally held content and cannot grow as later protection disappears.
Neither a manifest digest nor a pagination cursor authenticates either permission.

## Asset-side selection and protection manifest

An exact selection request is a small header over an existing immutable Tree.
Each indexed selection record names namespace/source/predecessor and a Tree of
exact logical paths with expected objects. The builder validates every binding
against its actual historical original root, owner and admitted Branch/Draft
class. Duplicate occurrences, missing paths, conflicting sizes, unresolved source
prepares and unavailable metadata refuse. No prefix, category, age, Git date or
file-name meaning is inferred. Native/age adapters retain their separate proofs.

The manifest header pins enrollment, request, complete coherent roster checkpoint
vector, cohort index, subject index, counts and paging policy. Each cohort stores
its exact original selection/context, owner/class/policy and selected-binding Tree.
Subject records distinguish surviving-claim protection from no remaining claim
observed after the whole exact selection. That is a hypothetical historical
projection, not live absence, verified custody, provider eligibility or authority.
It reads every admitted source class/root and unselected alias under aggregate
limits and reobserves the basis before returning a candidate. Ordinary bounded
bulk projection retains its original per-root exclusion bound; this separate
manifest projection has its own larger admitted aggregate bound.

All expanding inventories and record bodies stay asset-side. Small canonical
records remain at most64KiB; no giant portable JSON plan or Git inventory. Existing
Tree/NodeStore ports retain sharing; metadata provider finish/readback/adoption
remain explicit responsibilities. No payload is read or staged by compilation.
Known incomplete scope or resource exhaustion refuses instead of truncating.

Inspection verifies canonical records, request/cohort/subject identity and exact
selected subset/count consistency with original metadata. Its report is historical,
not a source lease or authentication of an arbitrary supplied header. Before any
future intent is admitted, the trusted controller must reconstruct the exact
manifest against independently current sources/policy/minima and compare its root.
After effects begin, replay uses acknowledged page transitions and fenced history,
not a fresh mutable query that silently redefines selection or eligibility.

## Stable original-binding pages

Paging order is deterministic occurrence identity followed by exact logical path.
The frozen policy caps binding count and canonical page bytes. A single cohort may
span pages. A cursor binds the manifest and an actual last occurrence/path; foreign
or nonexistent positions refuse. A cursor is an inspection position, not execution
progress, proof that earlier pages completed, or permission to skip them.

Each page names original bindings. It is deliberately not a ready-to-apply subset
plan against a source that an earlier page may already have replaced. Execution
must translate those exact unchanged paths through its acknowledged owned subset
lineage, preserve unselected paths and use fresh conditional subset plans. Never
keep applying requests against an original predecessor after its retirement.

Age evidence is inherited only through the existing verified subset protocol.
Finish/read back and acknowledge the inherited observation before a later page
depends on it. A crash after subset settlement but before inheritance resumes from
the exact original plan/receipts; matching hashes cannot replace that proof. New
independent occurrences never enter this historical selection automatically.

## Required controller after the manifest layer

Before any selected claim changes, acknowledge the full immutable selection and
separate decision: exact claim scope, original held/eligible content/copies,
original provider births/grants/plans, complete basis, admitted native/age proofs,
policy/context, authority/epoch and validity window. Bind deterministic operations
without self-hash cycles. A changed preview is a new decision, never an expanded
old consent. Provider eligibility cannot be inferred from projection alone.

Use existing accounted witness publication, capacity admission, scoped attempt
fences and source/subset/final-provider commands. Do not create a mutable SQL page
engine or append large runtime inventories to Git. Reserve intent/control/recovery
capacity before effects and exact bounded page/action metadata before its work;
never finance admission with prospective reclaim. A global atomic completion or
unbounded preallocation promise is not made. Unknown spent/missing evidence holds.

Per-page durable intent binds original page, exact current survivor lineage,
conditional subset plans and any age inheritance obligations before source IO.
Acknowledged completion follows verified actual transitions, not cursor movement.
The allowed-command history is derived only from these admitted immutable plans.
Unknown intervening source use, even a prepare/abort later withdrawn, invalidates
pending destructive work. Validate history under the final live subject fences;
the manifest checkpoint is not a lease. Stop further forward work on invalidation
while preserving completed effects and cancellation/recovery access.

Derive each immutable page-intent/completion slot from the admitted decision and
original page position. Serialize competing controllers only under that batch's
existing scoped control fence. Discover progress by these bounded exact slots and
receipts, never by trusting a caller's cursor or scanning arbitrary cache paths.
Use existing accounted publication/admissions for the slots, with no new mutable
SQL page journal. Domain-separated action identities must bind the fixed decision
inputs without recursively hashing their own resulting records.

Physical actions can progress once all selected aliases for an originally approved
subject have settled; other unselected/current/foreign claims still hold final
clearance. Invoke the existing original-birth/grant provider plan with current
authority/clock/window, source and provider fences. Old retries must spare a fresh
same-hash birth. Objects held at original approval never become deletion candidates
in this operation. A new decision is required for newly eligible content.

Cancellation durably stops future work, including page settlement. Report already
retired history, retained-but-present bytes, released grants and completed loss
separately; never invent rollback. Time/metadata-offline cancellation must retain
the existing recovery distinctions. Status is writer/time-free and distinguishes
historical effects, current observations, unresolved stores and cancellation debt.
