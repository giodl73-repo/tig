## Fresh authority, retained original meaning

The original paged decision approves its fixed historical selection and original
eligible action set. Claim settlement can finish while a copy remains held; the
original predecessor can then disappear. A later provider recovery or ended job
cannot enlarge that original action set or re-create the predecessor to make an
old selector work. The follow-up is a new operation with a new decision and new
private final-loss approval. It performs no source commands and inherits no old
permission to delete a newly observed placement.

Require an original completed or cancelled global witness under its exact owner,
enrollment, metadata identity, witness job and sorted original store-job bindings.
An unexecuted proposal, bare hash or request-supplied receipt is insufficient.
An active original intent must finish or be cancelled before follow-up admission;
it cannot keep issuing source transitions concurrently with its successor.
Cancelled partial settlement is admissible provenance, not proof that all original
claims were released. Current protection still decides whether any copy can proceed.

The retained original manifest/subjects constrain exact object digest and size.
Requested copies must lie within that original object scope and consenting owner.
Freeze each new actual provider plan, birth, grant set, capacity, exclusions and
current all-consumer basis into a bounded candidate. A fresh explicitly approved
decision may name a different exact placement/birth within that object scope;
neither the old final-loss approval nor equality of a content hash permits it.
Provider hard/foreign grants and current source/job/retention obligations hold.
Do not infer absence from target-place demand or incomplete source coverage.

Original age policy, when present, remains pinned and independently admitted.
Reverify retained exact evidence with the enrolled conservative clock; missing or
corrupt provenance remains unknown. An original age timestamp is not automatic
proof of project continuous inactivity after reactivation. Native writer/policy
integration must retain that distinction. No fact or source history is recreated.

## Existing durable ports and bounded execution

Stage expanding original references, requested copies, held/eligible explanations
and provider plans asset-side through existing Trees/NodeStore. A small canonical
header binds those indexes, original outcome, fresh source basis, operation,
policy, context and window. Finish and independently read back under one metadata
Session before acknowledging a candidate. Hashes authenticate no private authority.

Reuse the existing fixed accounted witness documents and final-provider intent,
grant release, unlink, completion and cancellation. Bind original provenance jobs
separately from new execution jobs. Preserve all acknowledged records and debits;
no prospective reclaim finances metadata or resets a reservation. Pin new frames
with independent vectors before accepting any persistent format.
Copy-only execution uses a metadata reader and admits provider resources only
for attempted actions within the run limit; it does not pre-admit an expanding
batch in front of a one-action call. Peak reports retain their named witness-only basis.

Before each fresh removal, hold current complete content-wide clearance and the
provider's exact object/birth/grant fence together. A source transition outside
the newly frozen basis invalidates remaining consent, including transient use.
Known completed provider outcomes remain historical; they cannot finance replayed
deletion of a later same-hash acquisition. Originally held follow-up rows stay
fixed too; later changes require another new decision and approval.

Apply/resume use the actual enrolled clock, exact private approval and finite
action/read/write/byte/time limits. Status/inspection use readers and no clock.
Cancellation acknowledges its own non-removable witness stop before metadata,
source, provider, registry or clock cleanup IO. Missing metadata cannot prevent
that stop; unknown cleanup and irreversible completed effects remain explicit.
All portable documents/records/responses stay at most64KiB; inventories stay
asset-side. No new global cache lock or independent SQL/journal engine.

## Required closure cases

- Provider unavailable in the original decision returns after claim settlement:
  old replay preserves its exclusions; fresh approval removes only the admitted copy.
- A foreign job keeps a shared object, then ends: fresh follow-up respects the
  whole current roster, and old/source-retired selections are not reconstructed.
- New/current/unknown protection, foreign grants, birth changes after planning, stale approval,
  weakened minima, changed scope or missing original witness/evidence refuse or hold.
- Cancelled partial origin and partial follow-up deletion preserve exact effects;
  process exits and missing/corrupt metadata cannot restart cancelled work or invent zero debt.
- Manual and aged original frames, ordinary bulk semantics and provider legacy
  refusal remain compatible. New frame readers/writers have explicit boundaries.

Metadata retirement must retain the original scope/outcome dependencies needed
by unfinished follow-ups, or use a separately admitted bounded successor proof.
That lifetime/expiry/authority handoff, realistic large-file/provider/latency,
agent/UI/Action integration and human intended-use acceptance remain required.
